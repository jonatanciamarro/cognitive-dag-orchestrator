# Organic Free Cycle

This example shows the free acquisition loop:

1. Research a topic with search intent.
2. Draft content for one primary channel and one repurposed channel.
3. Publish or schedule through allowed free channels.
4. Capture the opt-in lead on a landing page.
5. Write the result to memory.
6. Reuse the winning angle in the next cycle.

## Important Rule

No paid media. No spam. No skipped consent gate.
